## Program: Greatest Common Divisor

## What the program does
-Computes the greatest common divisor between integers a and b and prints it

## Register map
Register   |  What it holds
----------------------------
t0         |  address of a
t1         |  addres of b
t2         |  value of a
t3         |  value of b
t4         |  value of a%b

## Control flow
main -> gcd -> done
